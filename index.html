Key benefits
  • Helps reduce hair fall: nourishes the roots so less hair ends up on your comb.
  • Stronger roots: bhringraj and kalonji, trusted in Ayurveda for generations.
  • Healthier scalp: warm mustard oil soothes a dry, tight scalp.

  • Thicker-looking hair: rosemary and castor oil for fuller-looking strands.
  • Natural shine: soft, smooth hair without silicones or mineral oil.
How to use
  1. Take oil in your palm. About 1–2 teaspoons, warmed between your hands.
  2. Massage into the scalp. Part your hair and apply to the roots with slow, circular strokes.
  3. Leave it on 1–2 hours, or overnight for better results.
  4. Wash with a mild shampoo.
  5. Repeat 2–3 times a week for 3–4 weeks to see the difference.

Do a patch test behind your ear 24 hours before first use. For external use only.
