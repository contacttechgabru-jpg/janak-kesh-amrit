Shipping Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Where we ship

We ship to all serviceable pincodes across India.

Shipping charges

Shipping is free on every order, with no minimum order value.

Dispatch and delivery time

Orders are dispatched within 1–2 working days of successful payment. Delivery usually takes 3–7 working days depending on your location. Remote areas may take longer.

Tracking

Once your order is shipped, we send the courier name and tracking number on WhatsApp to the mobile number you gave at checkout.

Wrong address or failed delivery

Please check your address and pincode carefully before paying. If a parcel comes back to us because of an incorrect address or because nobody was available, we can re-ship it once you confirm the correct details. Re-shipping may be charged.
